# Sprout 2.0 — First Investment Journey

## About the Project

Sprout 2.0 is a UI/UX design concept for a mobile investing platform aimed at first-time investors in India. The goal is to make investing easier to understand through a simple interface, beginner-friendly educational content, and a guided onboarding experience.

The design introduces investing from ₹100 and helps users understand the basics, prepare for KYC, and explore their next steps.

## Design Approach

The design focuses on making investing feel approachable rather than intimidating. Clear messaging, simple layouts, and prominent calls to action help users understand what to do next without overwhelming them with financial terminology.

The desktop and mobile experiences share a consistent visual identity while adapting their layouts to different screen sizes.

## Visual Design

- Primary background: Deep Navy (#101D35)
- Accent color: Emerald Green (#16A36A)
- Typography: Inter
- Style: Minimal, modern, and beginner-friendly

## Mascot Concept

The Sprout mascot represents learning, personal growth, and confidence. It gradually grows from a small seedling into a tree across the onboarding screens, creating a visual connection between each stage of the journey.

The mascot symbolizes growth in knowledge and confidence, not guaranteed investment returns.

## Accessibility Considerations

- Readable typography and clear visual hierarchy
- Strong text contrast
- Interactive elements designed around a minimum 44 px touch target
- Visible investment risk disclaimers
- Simple content and straightforward navigation
- Flexible layouts to support future Hindi localization
